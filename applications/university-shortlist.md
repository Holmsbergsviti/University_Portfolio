# University shortlist: Computer Science (AI and security), entry September 2027

**Applicant:** based in Belgrade, Serbian (non-EU fees everywhere).
**Qualifications:** Cambridge International A Levels. AS 2026: Maths a, Computer Science a, Economics a, Physics b. A2 in Maths, CS and Economics in 2027. IELTS Academic 8.0, Writing 6.5.
**Researched:** 13 October 2026, from official university pages where they loaded. Check every figure on the course page before you apply. Items marked *(check)* could not be confirmed on an official page. Fees for 2027 are often not published yet; the last published figure is shown.

## How to read the tiers

- The tiers assume your school predicts about **A\*AA with A\* in Maths**, which fits AS grades of a/a/a. If your predictions are AAA, move every UK "match" up one tier.
- **Physics stopping at AS is not a problem anywhere on this list.** Computer Science counts as the science where one is required (Manchester, Royal Holloway, German rules).
- **No Further Maths** mainly hurts at Imperial and Warwick, which recommend it.
- **IELTS Writing 6.5 meets every minimum found.** It is exactly the floor at Manchester (6.5 in every part). Most universities only accept an IELTS certificate under 2 years old, so check its test date.

## Rules that shape the plan

- **UCAS allows 5 choices.** The table has 6 UK courses, so drop one. A balanced five is Imperial, Edinburgh, Manchester, Bristol and Royal Holloway, with Southampton swapped in for Bristol if you want a named cyber security degree.
- **Studielink allows at most 2 numerus fixus programmes.** Delft and Eindhoven are both numerus fixus, so they use up both slots.
- **Germany needs a C or better in all three A Levels.** A D in any one means no direct entry; the fallback is a foundation year (Studienkolleg). German deadlines fall before A Level results in August, so ask each university how it handles pending results.
- **Oxford and Cambridge are out for this cycle.** Their deadline is 15 Oct 2026.

## Documents you need

**For everyone**
- Passport
- IELTS Academic certificate (check it is still under 2 years old on the deadline)
- AS results statement for 2026 and predicted A Level grades
- GCSE/IGCSE results (the Dutch universities count them towards six subjects)
- School transcripts, with certified English translations of anything in Serbian

**UK (one UCAS application)**
- Personal statement: 4,000 characters in total, answering three questions (why this subject; how your studies prepared you; what you have done outside school), at least 350 characters each
- School reference with predicted grades
- TMUA result (Imperial, Warwick)

**Netherlands**
- English CV (TU Delft)
- Certified grade list and diploma uploaded to each university's portal
- Matching or selection activities (Delft, Eindhoven, Twente)

**Germany**
- CV and motivation letter
- Official Cambridge certificates (a school certificate alone is not enough)
- uni-assist check for THI
- SAT score or interview (Saarland)
- After an offer: a German national student visa, which needs a blocked account of about €11,900 a year
